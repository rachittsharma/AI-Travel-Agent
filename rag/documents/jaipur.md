# Jaipur Travel Guide & Royal Heritage

## Overview
Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is known as the "Pink City" due to its distinct terracotta-pink building facades. Part of India's famous Golden Triangle tourist circuit, Jaipur boasts grand hill forts, majestic royal palaces, rich handicrafts, and royal culinary heritage.

## Top Attractions & Experiences
- **Amber Fort (Amer Fort)**: Hilltop fortress featuring Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace), Rajput architecture, and panoramic lake views.
- **Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)**: Iconic 5-story pink sandstone palace with 953 intricate jharokhas (windows).
- **City Palace & Jantar Mantar**: Royal residence housing museums, and the nearby UNESCO World Heritage astronomical observatory.
- **Nahargarh Fort & Jaigarh Fort**: Great spots for dramatic sunset views over Jaipur city and viewing the world's largest cannon on wheels.
- **Bapu Bazaar & Johari Bazaar**: Famous for Rajasthani textiles, block prints, silver jewelry, and handcrafted mojaris (leather shoes).

## Best Time to Visit
- **Winter (Oct to Mar)**: Pleasant sunny days (15°C - 28°C) and cool evenings, ideal for fort exploration.
- **Summer (Apr to Jun)**: Extremely hot (up to 42°C); sightseeing restricted to early mornings/evenings.

## Culinary Highlights
- **Dal Baati Churma**: Quintessential Rajasthani dish of baked flour balls with lentils and sweetened crushed wheat.
- **Laal Maas**: Rich mutton curry prepared with Mathania red chillies and yogurt.
- **Pyaaz Kachori & Ghevar**: Famous crispy onion pastries and traditional disc-shaped disc dessert from Rawat Sweet Store.

## Transport & Travel Tips
- Auto-rickshaws, e-rickshaws, and Uber/Ola operate throughout Jaipur.
- Combined heritage monument composite tickets offer great discounts for multi-fort visits.
- Daily food & transport budget: ₹1,000 - ₹2,200 per person.
