# Kolkata & Chennai Destination Guides

## Kolkata — City of Joy & Colonial Heritage
Kolkata is the cultural capital of India, famous for Bengali literature, yellow taxis, tramways, colonial monuments, and grand festivals.
- **Top Attractions**: Victoria Memorial, Howrah Bridge, Dakshineswar Kali Temple, Park Street, Indian Museum, Princep Ghat.
- **Local Food**: Kathi Rolls at Nizam's, Rosogolla, Mishti Doi, Kosha Mangsho, Fish Fry.
- **Transportation**: Kolkata Metro (India's oldest), Yellow Taxis, Tramways, Hand-pulled rickshaws.

## Chennai — Gateway to South India
Chennai is Tamil Nadu's coastal capital, known for Marina Beach, Dravidian temples, classical Bharatnatyam dance, and South Indian coffee culture.
- **Top Attractions**: Marina Beach (2nd longest urban beach), Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Fort St. George, San Thome Basilica, Mahabalipuram (50 km).
- **Local Food**: Idli Sambhar, Crisp Masala Dosa, Chettinad Chicken Curry, Filter Coffee.
- **Transportation**: Chennai Metro, Suburban Rail, Auto-rickshaws.
- **Daily Budget**: ₹900 - ₹2,200 per person.
