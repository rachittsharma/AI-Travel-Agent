# Kerala Travel Guide — God's Own Country

## Overview
Kerala is a tropical state on India's Malabar Coast, famous for its network of palm-fringed backwaters, tea gardens in Munnar, wildlife sanctuaries, Ayurvedic wellness centers, and serene Arabian Sea beaches.

## Top Destinations & Attractions
- **Alleppey (Alappuzha) & Kumarakom**: Famous backwater cruises on traditional Kettuvallam houseboats.
- **Munnar**: Lush tea plantations, Eravikulam National Park (home to Nilgiri Tahr), and Mattupetty Dam.
- **Wayanad**: Spice plantations, Edakkal Caves, waterfalls, and eco-tourism.
- **Kochi (Cochin)**: Historic Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets, Jewish Synagogue, and Mattancherry Palace.
- **Kovalam & Varkala**: Cliffside ocean views, surf beaches, and Ayurvedic spa therapy.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season (Sep to Mar)**: Dry, warm, and comfortable weather (22°C - 32°C).
- **Monsoon (Jun to Aug)**: Best season for authentic Ayurvedic treatments and rejuvenation retreats.

## Local Food & Specialties
- **Appam with Stew**: Rice pancakes with soft centers served with coconut milk curry.
- **Kerala Sadya**: Traditional feast served on banana leaf with 20+ vegetarian dishes.
- **Karimeen Pollichathu**: Pearl spot fish marinated in spices and baked in banana leaves.

## Budget & Transport
- State transport buses (KSRTC) and local ferries offer economical transit across backwaters.
- Houseboat rentals range from ₹6,000 to ₹15,000 per night inclusive of meals.
- Daily food & transport budget: ₹1,500 - ₹3,500 per person.
