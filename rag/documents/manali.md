# Manali & Solang Valley Travel Guide

## Overview
Manali is a high-altitude Himalayan resort town in Himachal Pradesh, situated near the northern end of the Kullu Valley along the Beas River. Famous for adventure sports, snow-capped peaks, pine forests, and serene mountain cafes, it serves as a gateway to Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, and Leh-Ladakh.

## Top Attractions & Experiences
- **Solang Valley**: Adventure hub for paragliding, zorbing, quad biking, and winter skiing/snowboarding.
- **Rohtang Pass**: Located at 3,978 m, offering spectacular snow views and mountain passes (permit required).
- **Atal Tunnel & Sissu**: Scenic drive through the 9.02 km tunnel connecting Manali to Lahaul Valley and picturesque Sissu waterfalls.
- **Old Manali**: Bohemian vibe with wooden houses, hippymarket cafes, bakeries, and live acoustic music.
- **Hadimba Temple**: 16th-century wooden temple surrounded by dense cedar forests (Dhungri Van Vihar).
- **Vashisht Hot Springs**: Natural sulfur hot springs with therapeutic bath houses.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Summer (Mar to Jun)**: Ideal weather (10°C - 25°C) for sightseeing, paragliding, and trekking.
- **Winter (Dec to Feb)**: Heavy snowfall in Solang and upper Manali, perfect for skiing and snow lovers.

## Culinary Highlights & Local Food
- **Siddu**: Steamed wheat flour bread stuffed with poppy seeds, walnuts, and ghee.
- **Trout Fish**: Freshly caught river trout cooked in butter and local spices.
- **Himachali Dham**: Traditional festive feast served on leaf plates with curd, lentils, and rajma.
- **Cafes**: Try Himachali apple cider, wood-fired pizzas, and Israeli shwarma in Old Manali.

## Transportation & Local Tips
- Autoworkers and local taxis are readily available. Bus connects Manali to Delhi/Chandigarh overnight (12-14 hours).
- Permits for Rohtang Pass must be booked online in advance.
- Approximate daily food & transport budget: ₹1,200 - ₹2,800 per person.
