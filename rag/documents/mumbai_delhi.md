# Mumbai & Delhi Travel Guides

## Mumbai — The City of Dreams
Mumbai is India's financial capital, home to Bollywood, grand colonial architecture, coastal promenades, and buzzing street markets.
- **Top Attractions**: Gateway of India, Marine Drive (Queen's Necklace), Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, Elephanta Caves, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT), Juhu Beach, Bandra Fort.
- **Local Food**: Vada Pav, Pav Bhaji, Bhel Puri at Girgaon Chowpatty, Bombay Sandwich, Keema Pav at Irani cafes.
- **Transportation**: Mumbai Suburban Railway (local trains), Metro, Auto-rickshaws (Suburbs), Kaali-Peeli taxis.

## Delhi — Capital of Heritage & Modernity
Delhi seamlessly blends ancient Mughal and British colonial monuments with modern urban energy.
- **Top Attractions**: Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, India Gate, Lotus Temple, Akshardham, Chandni Chowk, Connaught Place.
- **Local Food**: Chole Bhature, Butter Chicken, Parathas at Paranthe Wali Gali, Dahi Bhalla, Kulfi.
- **Transportation**: Delhi Metro (world-class network), Uber/Ola, Auto-rickshaws.
- **Daily Budget**: ₹1,000 - ₹3,000 per day.
