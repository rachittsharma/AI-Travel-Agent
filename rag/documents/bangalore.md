# Bangalore (Bengaluru) Travel Guide — The Silicon Valley & Garden City

## Overview
Bangalore (Bengaluru) is the capital of Karnataka, known as the "Silicon Valley of India" and the "Garden City". Famous for its year-round pleasant climate, expansive parks, tech hubs, craft breweries, and vibrant rock & indie music culture.

## Top Attractions & Experiences
- **Lalbagh Botanical Garden & Cubbon Park**: Sprawling greenery, glass houses, and heritage trees.
- **Bangalore Palace**: Tudor-style royal residence built by the Wodeyar dynasty.
- **Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace**: Teakwood palace with ornate pillars and Mughal arches.
- **UB City & MG Road**: High-end shopping, luxury lounges, craft microbreweries, and art galleries.
- **Nandi Hills**: Popular early morning drive (60 km from city) for spectacular cloud beds and sunrise views.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Year-Round**: Mild temperatures (18°C - 30°C). Best months are October to February.

## Culinary Highlights & Local Food
- **Mascot Dosa & Filter Coffee**: Crisp Benne Dosa at CTR (Shri Sagar) or Vidyarthi Bhavan served with hot South Indian filter coffee.
- **Craft Beers**: Artisanal pale ales and IPAs at microbreweries like Toit, Arbor, and Byg Brewski.
- **Bisi Bele Bath & Mangalore Buns**: Traditional Karnataka spiced rice lentils dish and sweet banana bread.

## Transportation & Local Tips
- Namma Metro connects major IT corridors, MG Road, and Indiranagar.
- Daily food & transport budget: ₹1,200 - ₹3,000 per person.
