# Lucknow Travel Guide — City of Nawabs & Awadhi Delicacies

## Overview
Lucknow, the capital of Uttar Pradesh, is renowned as the "City of Nawabs". Famous for its elegant Awadhi architecture, Tehzeeb (refined etiquette), Chikankari embroidery, and world-class street food heritage.

## Top Attractions & Experiences
- **Bara Imambara & Bhool Bhulaiya**: Architectural marvel built in 1784 by Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula featuring an incredible labyrinth and gravity-defying vaulted hall.
- **Chota Imambara & Rumi Darwaza**: Ornate gold-domed monument and 60-foot grand gateway modeled after Istanbul's Sublime Porte.
- **British Residency**: Historic ruins of the 1857 siege surrounded by serene botanical lawns.
- **Hazratganj & Chowk**: Iconic shopping boulevards for authentic Chikankari suits, royal perfumes (Itthar), and antique jewelry.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Winter (Oct to Mar)**: Crisp, sunny, and pleasant weather (10°C - 24°C) ideal for heritage walks.

## Culinary Highlights & Local Food
- **Galouti & Tunday Kebabs**: Melt-in-mouth spiced minced mutton/beef kebabs served with Mughlai Parathas.
- **Basket Chat**: Crispy potato basket filled with tangy chutneys, sprouts, and curd at Royal Cafe in Hazratganj.
- **Awadhi Biryani & Sheermal**: Fragrant saffron-infused dum biryani and sweet saffron flatbread.
- **Kulfi Falooda & Prakash Kulfi**: Creamy saffron pistachio kulfi in Aminabad.

## Transportation & Local Tips
- Lucknow Metro connects Airport, Railway Station, and Hazratganj cleanly.
- Auto-rickshaws and Uber operate everywhere.
- Daily food & transport budget: ₹800 - ₹2,000 per person.
